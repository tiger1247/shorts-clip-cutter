# Shorts Clip Cutter – Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 17 November 2026_

Shorts Clip Cutter is a Windows desktop app made by **Nudge4**. This policy explains what data the app handles.

## The short version
- **The developer does not collect any data.** There is no account with us, no analytics, no tracking and no ads.
- Everything the app does happens **on your PC** or **directly between your PC and the services you choose to use**.

## What stays on your PC
- Your settings, clip lists, upload queue and styles: `%APPDATA%\ShortsClipCutter`
- Your YouTube sign-in token and Instagram access token (only if you connect them): same folder
- The videos, thumbnails and captions you create: the folder you choose
- Speech-recognition models (downloaded once, used offline): your user cache folder

You can delete all of it at any time by removing those folders. "Disconnect" on the Accounts tab removes your logins.

## Services the app talks to (only when you use the matching feature)
| Service | Why | What is sent |
|---|---|---|
| The video site you paste a link from (e.g. YouTube, Kick) | to read the video sections, comments and replay data you ask for | the video link |
| Google / YouTube Data API | to upload / schedule videos to **your** channel | your videos, titles, descriptions, thumbnails – using **your own** Google Cloud key |
| Meta / Instagram Graph API | to post Reels to **your** account | your videos and captions – using **your own** Meta app |
| Gumroad (Gumroad version only) | to check your license key | your license key |
| PyPI (pypi.org) | to update the built-in download engine | nothing personal (a version check) |
| Hugging Face | to download the speech-recognition model once | nothing personal |

These services have their own privacy policies (Google, Meta, Gumroad, etc.).

## Children
The app is not directed at children under 13.
